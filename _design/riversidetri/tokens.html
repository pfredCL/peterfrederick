Tri-Wrathlon timer: design tokens

Draft for your approval. Every colour, size and space in the app comes from these values in tokens.css. Nothing on this page is a real screen yet.

Who it's for

  • Station volunteers use their own phones at the boathouse, the stadium entrance and the stadium exit. They work outdoors, often one-handed, for a few hours once a year, repeating one action fast. So the station screens have big targets, high contrast and as few steps per competitor as possible.
  • You use a laptop at the boat club to start flights, fix entries and screenshot the results for the email. Those pages can be denser.
  • No spectators. The results page is designed to screenshot cleanly at laptop width.

Colour

One accent, Riverside blue, plus warm neutrals that look like a printed results sheet. Red is used only for errors. The ratios are contrast against the page background, worked out live on this page.

      Type

      Overpass for everything. It's an open version of Highway Gothic, the lettering on the Storrow and Memorial Drive signs along the run, drawn to be read at a glance outdoors. Times and numbers use its tabular figures, so they line up in columns without a second, monospaced font. Two weights only, 400 and 600.

      Spacing and radii

      Spacing comes in 4px steps. There are two radii: 4px for inputs and small buttons, and 10px for the big record button and dialogs. Nothing is pill-shaped. Only overlays get a shadow; rows and panels are separated by hairlines.

      In context

      The same pieces in both themes, using times from 2025 with made-up names. The numbers are placeholders too. This shows the tokens at work, not the screen layouts; those come in the mockups.

      Motion and icons

      • Pressing scales to 0.97 over 100ms. Try the record buttons above.
      • Interface transitions take 160ms. Overlays start at 0.95 with a fade, never from zero.
      • Recording a finish, the action used most, doesn't animate beyond the press itself.
      • Hover styles apply only on devices with a real pointer. With reduced motion turned on, scaling goes and fades stay.

      Icons are Lucide at a 2px stroke, 20px in buttons and 24px on their own. They appear only where they add meaning.
