Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 August 2026

What it accesses

With Google account permissions granted explicitly by the account holder, the application reads:

  • Gmail, read-only. Message text, senders, recipients, dates, and labels.
  • Google Calendar, read-only. Event titles, times, and attendees.

It has no ability to send mail, delete anything, or change any setting in these accounts. Attachments and images are not downloaded or stored — only a note that an attachment was present, with its filename.

Limited Use

This application's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
